(KPL) The Lancang-Mekong Media Dialogue, held in Xining and Yushu, Qinghai Province, China, brought together media representatives, government officials and experts from the six Lancang-Mekong countries to exchange views on strengthening media cooperation and promoting mutual understanding.

Speaking at the dialogue, Ms. Sivanxay Siphankham, Deputy Director General of the Lao News Agency (KPL), highlighted the important role of Lao media in Lancang-Mekong cooperation and called for closer professional exchanges among media organisations.

She recalled that Laos hosted a Lancang-Mekong media event in Vientiane in 2018, which provided opportunities for journalists to exchange experiences and strengthen professional networks.
